LongMemEval-S frozen benchmark inputs, 2026-10-18

This package contains public LongMemEval-S text transformed into a Munin SQLite
snapshot, session queries, and generated BGE corpus/query vectors. It contains
no production Munin memory, credentials, model weights or model cache.

Dataset: xiaowu0162/longmemeval-cleaned, revision
98d7416c24c778c2fee6e6f3006e7a073259d48f (dataset card declares MIT).
